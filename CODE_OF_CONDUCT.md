# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project and our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and
healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility for our mistakes, apologizing to those we affected, and learning from the experience.
- Focusing on what is best for the community, not only for ourselves.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject
comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and they
may temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behavior they judge inappropriate, threatening,
offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other
project communication), and also applies when a person represents the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers through GitHub's private reporting form linked in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md): https://github.com/amitverma-cf/agent.cpp/security/advisories/new
Describe what happened, where, and who was involved. Do not post the report in a public issue.

All reports are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated to respect the
privacy and security of the reporter. Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in
good faith may face consequences decided by the other maintainers.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers follow these guidelines when deciding how to respond to a violation:

1. **Correction.** Behavior that is unprofessional or unwelcome, with little impact. The response is a
   private message that explains the problem and asks for the behavior to stop. A public apology may be asked for.
2. **Warning.** A violation with more impact, or a pattern of behavior. The response is a warning and a
   period of no interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited contact.
3. **Temporary Ban.** A serious violation, or a continued pattern after a warning. The response is a
   temporary ban from interacting with the community.
4. **Permanent Ban.** A pattern of violations, or a single serious violation such as harassment of an
   individual or aggression toward a group. The response is a permanent ban from the project spaces.
